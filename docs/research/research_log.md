## What the research changes about the design

- **From #1:** availability must be derived per unit age, so `BLOOD_UNIT.expiry_date` is a stored, indexed column and `c_fefo_units` orders by it — never a quantity counter.
- **From #2 and #5:** stock-outs are a clinical safety event, so a short-stock condition raises an exception rather than returning zero silently.
- **From #3 and #4:** reporting must be by group × component × centre, not a national total, because the national total is already 99.66%.
- **From #6:** the gap is enforcement, so the business rules live in triggers and the package, and the APEX screen is not allowed to bypass them.
- **From #7:** the real data gives donor-level RFM, so donor recall is scored rather than broadcast.
- **From #8:** the "must refuse" rules are implemented as `BEFORE EACH ROW` triggers.
